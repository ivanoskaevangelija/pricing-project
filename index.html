FAQs

How does the price work?

Every boat-day has one price, set by the season and the day of the week, and it is lower when you book 30 or more days ahead. Saturdays, when most guests arrive or leave, are priced lower. The exact prices are in the Pricing section.

What does the deposit do?

A 30% deposit secures your date. Cancel 14 or more days before the trip and it is returned; cancel later and it is kept. If we cancel because of bad weather, you are refunded in full.

Can I get a better price at the marina on the day?

No. We keep one fair price for everyone, so there is no need to wait at the dock for a deal.

What is included?

A private sailing boat with a skipper for the day trip to the islands, 9:00 to 17:00, for up to 8 guests. Fuel, cleaning and marina fees are included.
